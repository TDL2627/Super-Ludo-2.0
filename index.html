🔥 Elemental Ludo 💧

Fire · Earth · Water · Air
How to play

Roll a six to leave base. Move exactly the number rolled. A six rolls again; three sixes in a row lose the turn. Land on a rival (not on ☆ or a start square, not shielded) to send it home. Reach the Nexus with an exact roll. Special tiles trigger only when you land on them: ☠ Chaos Rift sends that token home; +2 Wind Boost moves two more (skipped if it would overshoot, max 3 chained); ★ Elemental Star gives an extra roll; 🛡 Earth Guardian protects the token until it next moves. Keys: Space/R roll, 1–4 move token.
